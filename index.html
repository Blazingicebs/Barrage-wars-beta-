TRAINING GROUND

A quiet practice range — no zone, no waves, no other players. Stationary dummies stand around you and pop back up a couple seconds after you drop them. God Mode starts on so you can't die; toggle it off from the HUD if you want to test taking damage.

PLAYGROUND

Mess around with the zombie sandbox. Spawn a boss whenever you want. The playground boss has 1000× the health of a normal boss, because apparently ordinary amounts of health were too reasonable.

ZOMBIE MODE

A ruined city scorched into a volcanic wasteland, far from the island — lava-cracked streets, burning fire vents, and drifting embers. Zombies claw up out of the ground and always know exactly where you are — they will sprint straight at you. Survive wave after wave, each one bigger than the last. Clear a wave, or skip it to bring on more without losing the ones still standing.

CAPTURE THE FLAG

A separate walled-off map built for cover fighting — houses, crates and trees packed between two bases. Their flag sits guarded at the red base; grab it and get it back to your blue base to score. A squad of blue teammates drops in with you and will also push for their flag on their own. Watch your own flag too — red guards will try to steal it and run it back to their base, so kill the carrier to drop it. Get killed while carrying a flag and it drops on the spot — walk back over it to pick it up again, or it returns to base on its own after a while. First side to 3 captures wins.
